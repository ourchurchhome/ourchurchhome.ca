title: Combined Service — Sunday, August 30 in Morell

All three congregations of the Pastoral Charge will worship together on **Sunday, August 30, 2026 at 10:00 AM**.

The service will be held at **Peter Gordon Memorial United Church**, 14 Church St, Morell, PE.

This will be our only service that morning — St. Johns United Church in Mount Stewart and St. Peters Bay United Church will not hold their regular Sunday services on August 30. Everyone from all three congregations is warmly invited to join us in Morell.

Whether you worship with us every week or have not been in a while, you are welcome. We look forward to gathering as one community of faith.
