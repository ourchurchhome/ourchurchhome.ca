All three congregations of the Pastoral Charge will worship together.

The service will be held at **Mt Stewart.**

This will be our only service that morning — St. Johns United Church in Mount Stewart and St. Peters Bay United Church will not hold their regular Sunday services. Everyone from all three congregations is warmly invited to join us in Mt. Stewart.

Whether you worship with us every week or have not been in a while, you are welcome. We look forward to gathering as one community of faith.
